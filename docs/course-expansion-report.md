# Course expansion report

Completed 27 September 2026 in `cziwu12/nutrition-course-mvp`. All 24 lessons are published in Chinese and English. Week 1’s original lesson content is preserved. No weeks remain as developing content.

## Content and practice

Every week contains six substantial teaching sections, bilingual definitions, four recall questions with explanatory feedback, a practical task, a five-point summary and supporting references. Sections use original explanations, family examples, comparisons and callouts. Sources are optional background; the teaching is available inside the app. Medical topics explain limits and when individual professional advice is needed.

Weeks 17, 18, 19, 20, 23 and 24 provide saved worksheets with text export. The final guide has all 13 requested sections. Notes, answers, checklists and worksheet drafts survive refreshes and language changes. Legacy progress remains compatible. Reset requires confirmation.

## Sources and architecture

Primary sources include WHO, NIH ODS, NCCIH, NIDDK, NHLBI, the National Cancer Institute, Cochrane, NHS, Harvard Nutrition Source, KKM and the British Menopause Society. Sources are structured in topic-specific modules, with section references and derived week backlinks. Unverified library placeholders have been removed. Lesson prose, resources, videos and worksheets are data, separate from reusable interface components. No new backend, login or runtime translation dependency was introduced.
